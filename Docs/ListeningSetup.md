# Listening setup

Implemented 2026-11-05. After the optional demographic form closes, Step 2 shows an optional 6 kHz tinnitus-like example, draggable app-volume slider, Play/Stop, Accept and continue, and Skip. About you always appears first on every launch, followed by the volume step after the visitor closes it. About you on Home reopens this same sequence. No preview autoplays. Both popups block underlying screen controls and restore keyboard focus when closed. Demographic actions remain in a fixed footer while fields scroll.

For a fresh visit the slider starts at its midpoint, 0.125 in the unchanged 0–0.25 software range (displayed as 50%); press Play and drag gently after setting device volume low. Accept retains the chosen digital gain for this visit. Skip restores the gain used before opening setup. Reopening setup after accepting preserves the visit’s chosen gain. The gain applies equally to media comparison variants and game soundscapes, with a 0.25 software ceiling. It is temporary and resets with the visitor session. It cannot establish ear-level SPL or safe exposure. A single high-frequency tone is not a reliable calibration for music or broadband sound. Visitors who cannot hear it should skip rather than force audibility; stop if uncomfortable. These are application design constraints, not a WHO-approved calibration method.

The existing local mono 48 kHz PCM example is ten seconds with a dominant frequency of 6000 Hz; source peak 0.006012 and RMS 0.004249 were checked numerically. The runtime preview buffer alone receives linear headroom scaling to 0.25 digital peak (scale capped at 100) and 50 ms edge fades. Source files and comparison gains are unchanged. A preview-only factor of 10^(-12/20) = 0.25118864 reduces this example by 12 dB after the gain ramp. The slider range and gain for media/game sounds are unchanged. At the slider ceiling this preview peaks at approximately 0.01570 digital amplitude; no acoustic level follows from that value. Startup is ramped; Stop, Accept, Skip, reset, focus loss and destruction stop the preview. Preview loading is buffered with a 30-second timeout and an explanatory missing-file state.

Sources: [WHO safe listening](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/deafness-and-hearing-loss-safe-listening) for level/duration context; the supplied local example is educational, not a measured tinnitus percept or hearing test. Software checks do not establish physical headphone output or individual comfort. Physical listening remains unverified.
